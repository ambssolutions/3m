In this issue: what we build

Four kinds of work, each with its own crew, so you deal with people who do that job every week. Every job gets a written programme, a fixed price for the work in the contract, and one site supervisor who stays with it from the first visit to the final certificate.

01

New homes

From your plans or ours, on your block.

We build from your architect's drawings or help you get a design and approval for your block. You get a written programme, and the price is fixed for the work in the contract.

A good fit if

  • You have a block, or a house to knock down and rebuild
  • You have plans, or want help getting them drawn
  • You want the price fixed before work starts

Included

  • Design and approvals handled with your designer
  • Fixed-price building contract, written variations
  • Mandatory inspections booked with your certifier at every stage
  • Occupation certificate or final inspection applied for at the end

Approvals

  1. Before workDA from council, or CDC from a private certifier
  2. During the buildYour certifier inspects each mandatory stage
  3. At the endOccupation certificate or final inspection

02

Renovations and extensions

Kitchens, bathrooms, second storeys, re-cladding.

Kitchens, bathrooms, second storeys, decks and re-cladding. We stage the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom for as long as possible while we build.

A good fit if

  • You want more room without moving house
  • You plan to live at home while the work is done
  • The job changes walls, floors or the roof

Included

  • Staged so you can stay in the house
  • We tell you in advance when water or power is off
  • Dust barriers and floor protection as standard
  • Site swept and tidy at the end of every day

Approvals

  1. Before workWe check if it is exempt, or needs a CDC or DA
  2. During the buildCertifier inspections, where approval needs them
  3. At the endFinal certificate or inspection, if needed

Talk to us about renovations

03

Commercial fit-outs

Offices, retail and hospitality, built around your opening date.

Offices, retail and hospitality. We work to your lease dates and your opening date, including after-hours work where the building needs it.

A good fit if

  • You have signed a lease, or are about to
  • You have an opening date to work back from
  • Your building has rules on hours, access or noise

Included

  • Programme built around your opening date
  • Partition, ceiling, joinery and services trades coordinated by us
  • After-hours and weekend work where the building needs it
  • Landlord and building manager sign-offs handled

Approvals

  1. Before workLandlord sign-off, plus a DA or CDC if needed
  2. During the buildInspections, and fire and services certificates
  3. At the endOccupation certificate or final inspection to open

Talk to us about fit-outs

Light commercial

Warehouses, workshops and small multi-unit builds.

Warehouses, workshops, childcare centres and small multi-unit builds. We manage the engineering, the approvals and the subcontractors so you have one point of contact.

A good fit if

  • You are building a warehouse, workshop, childcare centre or a few units
  • You want one contact for engineers, approvals and trades
  • You have the land and know what the building is for

Included

  • One site supervisor for the whole job
  • Engineer and certifier inspections scheduled by us
  • Safe work method statements and a site safety plan for every job
  • Weekly progress report with photos

Approvals

  1. Before workDA from council, or CDC where the project qualifies
  2. During the buildEngineer and certifier inspections at each stage
  3. At the endOccupation certificate or final inspection

Built in layers, paid in stages

Most fixed-price home building contracts in Australia are paid in stages like these. Each stage is a finished layer of the house, and you pay for it once it is done.

01 Base
02 Frame
03 Lock-up
04 Fixing
05 Practical completion
  1. BaseSite set out, footings and the slab. Your certifier inspects the footings before the pour.
  2. FrameWall frames, roof trusses and bracing. The frame is inspected before the walls are lined.
  3. Lock-upRoof, external walls, windows and doors. The house can be locked.
  4. FixingLinings, cabinetry, wet area waterproofing and tiling, internal doors and trims.
  5. Practical completionFinal inspection, a walk-through with you, then the keys.

On the site supervisor

You get one site supervisor for the whole job. They walk the site with you at the start, run the crew every day, book the inspections and hand you the keys at the end. If something comes up a year later, they are still the person you call.

  • Our own carpenters. Framing, cladding and finishing are done by people on our payroll, not labour hire.
  • Licensed trades. The same electricians, plumbers and painters on every job, so the finish is consistent.
  • Weekly updates. A short report with photos every week, and a current finish date.
  • Tidy site. Swept at the end of every day, and skips emptied before they overflow.
