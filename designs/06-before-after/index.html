Residential or commercial?

Two sides of the business, each with its own crew. Open a side to see what we take on and what is included.

Residential

Homes

New homes on your block, and renovations and extensions to the one you have.

New homes

We build from your architect's drawings or help you get a design and approval for your block. You get a written programme, and the price is fixed for the work in the contract.

  • Design and approvals handled with your designer
  • Fixed-price building contract, written variations
  • Mandatory inspections booked with your certifier at every stage
  • Occupation certificate or final inspection applied for at the end

Renovations and extensions

Kitchens, bathrooms, second storeys, decks and re-cladding. We stage the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom for as long as possible while we build.

  • Staged so you can stay in the house
  • We tell you in advance when water or power is off
  • Dust barriers and floor protection as standard
  • Site swept and tidy at the end of every day
Commercial

Workplaces

Fit-outs for offices, retail and hospitality, and light commercial builds.

Commercial fit-outs

Offices, retail and hospitality. We work to your lease dates and your opening date, including after-hours work where the building needs it.

  • Programme built around your opening date
  • Partition, ceiling, joinery and services trades coordinated by us
  • After-hours and weekend work where the building needs it
  • Landlord and building manager sign-offs handled

Light commercial

Warehouses, workshops, childcare centres and small multi-unit builds. We manage the engineering, the approvals and the subcontractors so you have one point of contact.

  • One site supervisor for the whole job
  • Engineer and certifier inspections scheduled by us
  • Safe work method statements and a site safety plan for every job
  • Weekly progress report with photos

Things people ask us

Most new homes, extensions and structural changes need a development application through your council, or a complying development certificate through a private certifier. Smaller jobs can be exempt development. The rules differ by state and council. We tell you which applies at the first site visit, and we lodge it for you.

It depends on the design, the site, the approvals and the weather. We give you a written programme with the quote and update it at every stage, so you always know the current finish date.

The quote is a fixed price for the work described in it, under an industry-standard fixed-price building contract. Anything you add or change later is priced in writing as a variation before we do it, so there are no surprises on the final invoice.

Often, yes. We stage the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom for as long as possible, and we tell you in advance which days the water or power will be off.

We walk through the job with you, fix anything on the list, and apply for the occupation certificate or final inspection. You get the approval documents, compliance certificates, warranties and manuals in one folder.

The people behind the work

You get one site supervisor for the whole job. They walk the site with you at the start, run the crew every day, book the inspections and hand you the keys at the end. If something comes up a year later, they are still the person you call.

  • Our own carpenters. Framing, cladding and finishing are done by people on our payroll, not labour hire.
  • Licensed trades. The same electricians, plumbers and painters on every job, so the finish is consistent.
  • Weekly updates. A short report with photos every week, and a current finish date.
  • Tidy site. Swept at the end of every day, and skips emptied before they overflow.
