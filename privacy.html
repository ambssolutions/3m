Privacy statement.

This statement explains what 3M Construction does with the personal information you give us through this website. It follows the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you send the quote form we receive your name, phone number, email address and whatever you write about your job, including the suburb and budget if you add them. Nothing is collected until you press send.

What we use it for

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a site visit.
  • To prepare an estimate or quote if you ask for one.

We do not sell your details or add you to a marketing list.

Who sees it

Your enquiry goes to our office email. If the form is sent through a form service, that service handles the message in transit and does not keep it for its own use. We do not share your details with anyone else unless you ask us to, for example when we arrange a designer or engineer for your job.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are talking to you about the job, and for our business records afterwards. Ask us and we will delete them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies. If we add analytics later, we will say so here.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Use the contact details on the home page. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
