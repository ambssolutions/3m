Why we suggest A

It is the simplest of the three: one line, nothing added, and it still reads as 3M at the size of a browser tab. The two roof pitches say homes and building without a picture, and a single line is easy to paint on a ute door, cut into a site sign or stitch on a shirt. B is the strongest as an app or tab icon. C has the most character but needs more room to read.

  • Every line is drawn on a grid as simple shapes, so the logo is sharp at any size and does not depend on a font.
  • The colours come from the colour scheme, so the logo changes with it, including the night scheme.
  • The build-up plays once, in under two seconds. People who turn off motion on their device see the finished logo straight away.
  • The shapes, wording and colours can all be adjusted before anything is final.
