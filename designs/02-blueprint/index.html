A-05

Who is on the drawing

You get one site supervisor for the whole job. They walk the site with you at the start, run the crew every day, book the inspections and hand you the keys at the end. If something comes up a year later, they are still the person you call.

  • Our own carpenters. Framing, cladding and finishing are done by people on our payroll, not labour hire.
  • Licensed trades. The same electricians, plumbers and painters on every job, so the finish is consistent.
  • Weekly updates. A short report with photos every week, and a current finish date.
  • Tidy site. Swept at the end of every day, and skips emptied before they overflow.

A-06

General notes

The questions we are asked most, answered the way we would on site.

  1. Most new homes, extensions and structural changes need a development application through your council, or a complying development certificate through a private certifier. Smaller jobs can be exempt development. The rules differ by state and council. We tell you which applies at the first site visit, and we lodge it for you.

  2. It depends on the design, the site, the approvals and the weather. We give you a written programme with the quote and update it at every stage, so you always know the current finish date.

  3. The quote is a fixed price for the work described in it, under an industry-standard fixed-price building contract. Anything you add or change later is priced in writing as a variation before we do it, so there are no surprises on the final invoice.

  4. Often, yes. We stage the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom for as long as possible, and we tell you in advance which days the water or power will be off.

  5. We walk through the job with you, fix anything on the list, and apply for the occupation certificate or final inspection. You get the approval documents, compliance certificates, warranties and manuals in one folder.
