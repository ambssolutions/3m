What we build

Four kinds of work, each with its own crew, so you deal with people who do that job every week.

New homes

We build from your architect's drawings or help you get a design and consent for your section. You get a written programme, and the price is fixed for the work in the contract.

  • Design and consent handled with your designer
  • Fixed-price contract, written variations
  • Council inspections booked at every stage
  • Code compliance certificate applied for at the end

Renovations and extensions

Kitchens, bathrooms, second storeys, decks and re-cladding. We stage the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom for as long as possible while we build.

  • Staged so you can stay in the house
  • We tell you in advance when water or power is off
  • Dust walls and floor protection as standard
  • Site swept and tidy at the end of every day

Commercial fit-outs

Offices, retail and hospitality. We work to your lease dates and your opening date, including after-hours work where the building needs it.

  • Programme built around your opening date
  • Partition, ceiling, joinery and services trades coordinated by us
  • After-hours and weekend work where the building needs it
  • Landlord and building manager sign-offs handled

Light commercial

Warehouses, workshops, childcare centres and small multi-unit builds. We manage the engineering, the council and the subcontractors so you have one point of contact.

  • One site manager for the whole job
  • Engineer and council inspections scheduled by us
  • Health and safety plan for every site
  • Weekly progress report with photos

How a job runs

Five stages. Each one says what we deliver and who signs it off.

01

Site visit and estimate

Week 1
  • We walk the site with you and talk through what you want
  • You get a written estimate with a range, and a list of what would change it
  • No charge, and no obligation
Who signs it off
You
What you get
Estimate and a draft programme
02

Plans and building consent

Design to consent
  • Your designer or ours draws the plans, and we price them line by line
  • We lodge the building consent application with the council
  • Fixed-price contract signed once the consent is issued
Who signs it off
Auckland Council issues the building consent
What you get
Consented plans and a fixed-price contract
03

The build

On site
  • One site manager, the same person from start to finish
  • Weekly progress report with photos
  • Anything you change is priced in writing before we do it
  • Council inspections at foundations, framing, pre-line and completion
Who signs it off
Council inspector at each inspection
What you get
A weekly update and a current finish date
04

Walk-through and handover

Practical completion
  • We walk through the finished job with you and write down anything to fix
  • Those items are done before the final invoice
  • Keys, warranties and manuals handed over in one folder
Who signs it off
You
What you get
The keys, and the warranties
05

Code compliance certificate

After handover
  • We apply to the council for the code compliance certificate and chase it until it is issued
  • Producer statements and as-built drawings supplied with the application
  • Defects period: anything that comes up, you call your site manager
Who signs it off
Auckland Council issues the certificate
What you get
The certificate, and a builder you can still call

Who you deal with

You get one site manager for the whole job. They walk the site with you at the start, run the crew every day, book the council inspections and hand you the keys at the end. If something comes up a year later, they are still the person you call.

  • Our own carpenters. Framing, cladding and finishing are done by people on our payroll, not labour hire.
  • Trusted trades. The same electricians, plumbers and painters on every job, so the finish is consistent.
  • Weekly updates. A short report with photos every week, and a current finish date.
  • Tidy site. Swept at the end of every day, and skips emptied before they overflow.
